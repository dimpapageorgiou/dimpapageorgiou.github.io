## About the project

ShippingLab is a large-scale maritime research and innovation initiative aimed at strengthening Denmark’s position in the global maritime sector through digitalisation, autonomous shipping, and decarbonisation. The project brings together maritime companies, technology suppliers, ship operators, and research institutions to develop and demonstrate technologies for the future ship. Its activities are organised around digital ship operations, autonomous vessel technologies, and low- and zero-emission power solutions, supported by programme-wide integration and knowledge sharing. The project combines digital twins, predictive maintenance, autonomous navigation and supervision, and full-scale testing of alternative propulsion and power technologies in operational maritime environments.

## Objectives

- Advance digital ship operations through digital twins, predictive analytics, and data-driven vessel optimisation.
- Develop and demonstrate autonomous shipping technologies for safer and more efficient vessel operations.
- Develop and test decarbonisation technologies, including hydrogen fuel cells and maritime power systems.
- Establish open standards and integrated digital solutions for maritime data and autonomous systems.
- Strengthen maritime innovation and commercialisation through industry–research collaboration.
