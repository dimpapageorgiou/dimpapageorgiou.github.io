## About the project

ShippingLab Green (SLGreen) aims to accelerate the green transition of the maritime sector by developing and demonstrating digital technologies that improve energy efficiency, decarbonisation, safety, and working conditions at sea. The project brings together maritime companies, technology providers, and research institutions to develop solutions spanning vessel performance and biofouling prediction, engine condition monitoring, remote digital pilotage, and crew health and comfort. Together, these technologies are intended to reduce fuel consumption and CO2 emissions while strengthening the competitiveness and technological capabilities of the Danish maritime sector.


## Objectives

- Develop digital twins and prediction tools for vessel performance to improve vessel design, navigation, and energy efficiency under real-world wave and weather conditions.
- Develop biofouling prediction models to support hull-performance management and reduce the additional fuel consumption and emissions caused by fouling.
- Develop condition-monitoring and wear-prediction models for marine engines to enable predictive maintenance, improve reliability, and optimize vessel operation.
- Develop digital pilotage technology for remote navigation, improving the efficiency and safety of vessel operations in coastal and confined waters.
- Develop digital decision-support tools for crew health and comfort, particularly to predict and mitigate seasickness aboard crew-transfer vessels.
