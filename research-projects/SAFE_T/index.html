SAFE-T

Singularity-aware framework for estimation via topology shaping

2026–2029 Principal Investigator Research Project 2 All research projects →

About the project

In engineering applications, it is often necessary to invert nonlinear mappings to extract information that is latent in measured signals. Many inversion methods involve mathematical operations (e.g. inversion of matrices, divisions, calculation of gradients etc.), in which singularities may emerge. Such singularities may also evolve with time, creating dynamic unsafe regions for estimation, like moving obstacles. Moreover, there may be cases where the parameter space may be fragmented into disjoint subsets.

SAFE-T investigates the safe inversion of nonlinear mappings that arise in control and diagnosis applications for complex systems.

The project aims at developing a novel, generalisable theoretical and algorithmic framework for singularity-aware inversion of nonlinear mappings (asymptotic estimation), with applications in control & diagnosis relevant across diverse engineering domains.

Objectives

  • Development of a framework for safe and continuous estimation under singularities in connected or in disconnected spaces.
  • Formal integration of safe navigation principles into estimation laws, for avoiding dynamic unsafe regions.
  • Formal integration of geometric & learning-based lifting into estimation algorithms for inversion in disconnected spaces.
  • Development of distributed safe mapping inversion methods in multi-agent control systems.
