### Current Courses

**Linear Control Design 1** (BSc · 5 ECTS · Spring)<br>
This the first course in control theory. We learn about modelling, hand-tuning, transfer functions, frequency domain analysis and PI-Lead controller design. It is taught in collaboration with Prof. Silvia Tolu. Another version of the course is ran during fall semesters by Prof. Henrik Niemann.

<u>DTU course page</u> [34721](https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/34721), [34722](https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/34722), [MA722](https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/MA722)

**Robust and fault-tolerant control** (MSc · 10 ECTS · Spring)<br>
This an advanced MSc course in control theory. It is structured in two modules. In the first module we learn about fault detection and isolation based on structureal properties of the system. We employ state-space and frequency domain representations to generate signals that inform of occuring faults. We also use statistical change detection prinicples to robustify our conclusions against noise. Finally, we learn how to design fault-tolerant controllers. The second part concerns frequency domain robust controller synthesis for uncertain MIMO systems. It is taught in collaboration with Prof. Henrik Niemann.

<u>DTU course page</u> [34746](https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/34746)

**Topics in advanced control (Nonlinear systems analysis and control)** (PhD · 5 ECTS · Fall)<br>
This is an advanced PhD course in control theory. We address the stability and boundedness properties of pertutbed non-autonomous nonlinear systems via Lyapunov theory. We learn how to desing sliding-mode and adaptive controllers for uncertain systems and we employ online parameter estimation for condition monitoring and advanced cascaded control architectures. All students (PhD, MSc and BSc) are welcome.

<u>DTU course page</u> [34791](https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/34791)
